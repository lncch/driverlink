01 / THE PROBLEM Rubric 1 · Problem ما هي المشكلة؟

Two sides of the same market, and no place where they meet.

There is no single portal in Saudi Arabia for driver recruitment. The general job boards that exist carry almost no detail about the driver, and almost no detail about the employer.
Drivers
A driver whose contract just ended has no pipeline. They ask around, wait on word of mouth, and lose weeks to a search with no visibility into salary, route type, or housing before they commit.
Companies
Transport, delivery and logistics firms hire through personal networks, their own website, or a paid advertisement. That costs time, money and effort, and still doesn't reach the right candidate.
Result
Vacancies stay open, qualified drivers already in the Kingdom stay unemployed, and companies fall back on recruiting from outside Saudi Arabia instead.
02 / ROOT CAUSE Rubric 1 · Fishbone لماذا تحدث المشكلة؟

Why does it happen? Six causes, one effect.

The deepest cause sits in the middle two bones: the information a hiring decision needs (licence class, vehicle category, verified identity, real salary) is never captured in a structured, searchable form, so no channel can match on it.

03 / WHO IS AFFECTED Rubric 3 · Users من المتأثر؟

Who uses it, and who else has a stake.

Primary users

Drivers
Truck, bus, parcel-delivery, private and commercial drivers holding a valid Saudi licence. They search, save, apply and track from the mobile app.
Recruiters
Hiring staff and fleet managers at transport, delivery and logistics companies. They post vacancies, filter, and manage candidates on the website.
Small firms
Establishments with no HR department and no recruitment budget, currently limited to their own contacts.

Other stakeholders

HRSD
Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development. Licenses employment mediation platforms, and benefits from local hiring over overseas recruitment.
TGA
Transport General Authority. Sets Saudization rules, unified driver status, and operating licence conditions the platform must respect.
Providers
Nafath and Absher for identity, Wathq for commercial records, SMS gateways, payment gateway, cloud host. Dependencies, not customers.
04 / GOAL & OBJECTIVES Rubric 2 · Goal ما الذي نريد تحقيقه؟
Connect companies that need professional drivers with the drivers already in Saudi Arabia, through one trusted, verified platform.
IDObjectiveSuccess indicator
OBJ-1Provide an employment platform specialised for professional drivers.20 verified companies and 200 verified drivers registered within 3 months of launch.
OBJ-2Make it faster for a driver to find a suitable job.Median time from verified account to first submitted application under 10 minutes.
OBJ-3Help companies hire drivers already living in the Kingdom instead of recruiting from abroad.70% of closed vacancies filled by a driver already resident in Saudi Arabia.
OBJ-4Let companies create detailed, searchable vacancies.100% of published vacancies carry licence class, vehicle category, city, salary range and working hours; the form rejects a posting without them.
OBJ-5Cut the time it takes to hire a driver with the right qualifications.Median 7 days or fewer from posting to first shortlist, against each company's own pre-platform baseline recorded at sign-up.
OBJ-6Let drivers submit and track applications.Every application shows a timestamped state (Submitted, Viewed, Shortlisted, Rejected, Hired); 90% reach a final state within 30 days.
OBJ-7Reduce unsuitable applications by showing requirements before a driver applies.Under 15% of applications rejected for a requirement that was already stated in the posting.
05 / PROPOSED SOLUTION Rubric 4 · Solution ما هو الحل المقترح؟

One verified platform. Website for both sides, app for drivers.

Website and mobile application run on a shared database and backend, so a vacancy posted by a recruiter on the web appears in a driver's app immediately, and an application submitted on the app appears in the recruiter's pipeline.

IN-01

Registration & verification

Driver identity through Nafath and Absher, company commercial record through Wathq. Fake postings and unlicensed drivers never enter the pool.

IN-02

Job management

Companies post the details that actually decide a match: licence class, vehicle category, route, salary, benefits, working hours.

IN-03

Search & matching

Filter by licence type, city, experience and vehicle category, on both sides of the market.

IN-04

Application tracking

Drivers save jobs, get notifications, and see application state. Companies manage candidates in one pipeline.

IN-05

Mutual reviews

Companies rate drivers, drivers rate companies. The information asymmetry works in both directions, so the fix has to as well.

IN-06

Multilingual interface

Arabic and English across web and app, because the driver workforce in the Kingdom is not monolingual.

06 / SCOPE Rubric 4 · Scope حدود المشروع

DriverLink is a job board. It stops at the hire.

Inside the boundary

IN-01Profiles, ID and document verification
IN-02Vacancy posting and management
IN-03Search, filtering and job matching
IN-04Applications, saved jobs, notifications
IN-05Mutual review and rating
IN-06Multilingual web and mobile interfaces

Outside the boundary

EX-01Overseas recruitmentKingdom-resident drivers only, to avoid visa and transfer processes
EX-02Payroll and HR managementNo salaries, no shift scheduling after the hire
EX-03Signing legal contractsOfficial employment contracts happen outside the platform
EX-04Live GPS vehicle trackingFleet management, a different product
